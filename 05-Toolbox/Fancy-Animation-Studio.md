# Fancy Animation Studio — composing motion in the browser

**<https://fancyanimation-c1621.web.app>** — a free, browser-based animation
studio built around **motion paths**: you place vector elements and drive them
along bezier, arc, orbit, spiral, wave, and bounce paths, with easing control
per segment and blend modes for layered looks. No install, no license — it runs
where the web runs.

## Why it's in the course

- **Easing is front and center.** In Week 3 it's the easing lab: same move, different curve, feel the difference.
- **Motion paths as vocabulary.** Most UI motion is a straight line with an ease; knowing arcs, orbits, waves, and bounces expands what you can say.
- **Staging longer compositions.** Week 10 uses it to build 10–20 second staged sequences — a different muscle than a half-second microinteraction.
- **A lightweight tool in a pro pipeline.** Part of the lesson is judgment: what belongs in a quick browser tool, and what needs After Effects or Rive? ([[Platform-Comparison]])

## Where it appears

Week 3 (easing hands-on), Week 10 (deep dive), Project 3 (one of the two
platform options), and optionally the final project.

## Tips

- Work at the composition level: name elements, stage entrances/exits deliberately, and treat each path segment's easing as a design decision.
- Compare the same path drawn as bezier vs. arc vs. wave — the Week 10 exercise — and articulate the difference in feel, not just shape.
