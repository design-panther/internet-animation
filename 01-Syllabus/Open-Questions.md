# Open questions — syllabus revision (INTERNAL)

Carried over from the 16-week draft revision. Resolve before the syllabus is
final; the public wiki pages currently present the *proposed* resolutions.

1. **Course title conflict** — old file says "Multimedia Concepts," draft says
   "Internet Animation." The wiki uses **Internet Animation**. Confirm with the
   department that the catalog title matches.
2. **Hybrid format** — confirm the "no formal meeting time; ET 206 open
   8:00–9:15am T/TH" arrangement is still the current format. It also conflicts
   with the Art Department attendance policy as written; the public Policies
   page adds a "how this applies in a hybrid course" gloss — confirm that
   interpretation is acceptable to the department.
3. **After Effects availability** — AE was NOT on the old lab-software list but
   is required for the Bodymovin/Lottie pipeline as drafted. Confirm the
   current lab image includes it.
4. **Adobe Animate** — retired from this course, or kept for one
   legacy/comparison week? Currently retired (Week 1 covers the Flash→Animate
   history only).
5. **Premiere / Audition** — dropped from this revision. Confirm no
   department requirement remains.
6. **Grade scale** — the old syllabus scale (A=45–50 … F≤29) totals ~50 pts and
   its C/D ranges overlap. Structure updated 2026-08-19 per Reid: 4 projects
   (4×50) + **14 in-class exercises of which any 7 count**. The wiki publishes
   a 90/80/70/60% scale on **270 points** (A=243+, B=216–242, C=189–215,
   D=162–188, F<162), **assuming 10 pts per exercise** — confirm that value,
   and that the department doesn't mandate a different scale.
6a. **Exercise content** — the 14 exercise pages (03-Projects/Exercise-01…14)
   are PLACEHOLDERS only. Reid provides the instructions later; do not author
   exercise content. Which weeks each exercise is offered in is also TBD.
7. **Programming outcome** — old outcome "create interactive applications using
   basic programming commands" replaced with "develop simple interactive
   animations using state-based authoring tools" (Rive state machines). Confirm
   this satisfies the outcome on record.
8. **COVID-19 provisions** — removed from the public pages; replace with
   current university language if one is mandated.
9. **Flash-drive requirement** — old 32GB flash-drive requirement replaced
   with OneDrive (flash drive optional).
10. **Instructor info** — fill in Fairmont State email, office location, and
    office hours (currently omitted from the public syllabus).
11. **Semester dates** — the seeded class calendar assumes the Fall 2026
    semester starts Mon Aug 17, 2026 (Week 16 ends Dec 4). Verify against the
    academic calendar and fix the events if off.
