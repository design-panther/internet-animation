# Course & University Policies

## Art Department attendance policy

The Art Department at Fairmont State University has adopted a uniform
attendance policy for all students taking art courses:

- After **three** missed classes, the final grade drops one letter for each additional absence.
- Students who miss more than **six** classes for any reason during the semester will not pass the course.
- Because much of the skill building in studio courses occurs in the first weeks before midterm, students who miss more than **four classes before midterm** for any reason cannot pass the course. It is advised that students drop before the drop deadline.
- Regardless of whether an absence is legitimate, a missed class cannot be "made up" — demonstrations, guided practice, contact with other students, and critiques do not occur outside scheduled times.
- In cases of military service, serious illness, or a debilitating accident, a professor may grant an "Incomplete." This exception is granted only rarely, based on previous performance and regular attendance.

> **How this applies in a hybrid course:** for GRFX1222, "attendance" is
> measured by engagement — showing up to scheduled critiques (Weeks 8 and 16),
> making use of the T/TH open work sessions in ET 206, and hitting weekly
> deliverables. Missing a scheduled critique counts as an absence.

## Communication

The best method to contact the instructor is via **school Webmail** or in
person during office hours. Course communication happens only through the
school-issued email account — develop the habit of checking it frequently.
Course announcements go out via Webmail and/or Blackboard.

## Social Justice / Nondiscrimination

Fairmont State University is committed to social justice. I concur with that
commitment and expect to maintain a positive learning environment based upon
open communication, mutual respect, and non-discrimination. Fairmont State
University does not discriminate on the basis of race, sex, age, disability,
veteran's status, religion, sexual orientation, color or national origin. Any
suggestions for furthering a positive and open environment in this class will
be appreciated and given serious consideration.

**If you are a person with a disability** and anticipate needing any type of
accommodation in order to participate in this class, please advise the
instructor and make appropriate arrangements with the **Office of Accessibility
Services (304-367-4141)**.

## Lab etiquette

The computer labs and classrooms are used by numerous classes and students.
Take all your belongings with you when you leave your workstation. If your
belongings go missing, Wallman Hall 304 or the library circulation desk may be
able to help.
