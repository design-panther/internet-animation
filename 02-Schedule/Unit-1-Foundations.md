# Unit 1 — Foundations (Weeks 1–4)

The ground floor: why motion exists on the modern web, how to build vector
artwork that can actually be animated, the classic animation principles
translated to interface motion, and the User-Centered Design habit that runs
through the whole course — **motion has an audience and a job**.

- [[Week-01]] — Why Motion? The Landscape of Vector Animation
- [[Week-02]] — Vector Drawing Foundations
- [[Week-03]] — Principles of Animation for Vector & UI
- [[Week-04]] — UCD I: Research & Intent

**Deliverables:** [[Project-1-Animated-Asset-Kit]] (Week 4, 50 pts), plus
whichever in-class exercises are offered these weeks (any 7 of the semester's
14 count — see [[Grading]]).

**What "done" looks like:** you can draw a clean, layer-organized vector asset
set, animate it with easing you can defend, and hand over a one-page motion
brief that says who it's for and what it's supposed to do.

**Read:** the unit essay [[Why-Motion-Needs-A-User]] · resources tagged
`unit-1` in [[Learning-Resources]].
