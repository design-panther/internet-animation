# Unit 4 — Final Project (Weeks 13–16)

Everything at once: an approved proposal becomes a persona, a motion brief, and
a storyboard; production runs through a mid-course peer usability test; the
last week is revision, optimization, and portfolio packaging. Presentations
tell the whole UCD story — brief → test → revision — not just the pretty file.

- [[Week-13]] — Research & Design
- [[Week-14]] — Production I + Peer Testing
- [[Week-15]] — Production II: Iterate & Polish
- [[Week-16]] — FINAL PRESENTATIONS

**Deliverable:** [[Project-4-Final-Animation]] (Week 16, 50 pts).

**What "done" looks like:** finished animation(s) on the platform your brief
justified, optimized (file size + reduced-motion fallback), packaged on a
LottieFiles profile or demo page, presented with the process story.

`unit-4` in [[Learning-Resources]].
