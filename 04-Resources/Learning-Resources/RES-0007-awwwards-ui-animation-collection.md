## What it covers
A free, curated Awwwards collection of "UI Animation and Microinteractions" — over 240 real, shipped examples using Canvas, SVG, CSS3, and WebGL, spanning hover effects, page transitions, scroll interactions, and 3D animation from professional studios and independent designers.

## What you'll learn
- A wide sample set of what "great UI animation" looks like in the wild, right now
- How to distinguish types of interaction (hover vs. scroll-triggered vs. page-transition)
- Enough real examples to build a Week 1 "audit" assignment around (pick 3, say why they work or don't)
- Exposure to studios/designers working professionally in this space

## Notes
Assign as the "go find 3 examples and critique them" resource for Week 1's audit exercise. Note: Awwwards also sells a paid "Creative Pass" for video courses elsewhere on the site — this specific collection page is free to browse and does not require a login.
