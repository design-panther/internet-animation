## What it covers
Rive's own comparison of its tool against Lottie, covering interactivity (state machines vs. code-driven playback), file size, responsiveness/layout, GPU-accelerated performance, and workflow (a single editor vs. Illustrator/Figma + After Effects + Bodymovin). It positions Lottie as best for simple, linear marketing animations and Rive as best for state-driven, interactive graphics, citing Duolingo, Intercom, and Brilliant.org as adopters.

## What you'll learn
- The core technical difference: Lottie plays back a fixed timeline; Rive runs a state machine
- Concrete file-size and pipeline tradeoffs between the two formats
- When a team would reasonably choose Lottie over Rive, and vice versa
- How dotLottie's compression narrows (but doesn't erase) the file-size gap
- Vocabulary needed to discuss "Rive vs Lottie tradeoffs" as covered in this course

## Notes
This is a vendor blog, so specific numbers (e.g. "10-15x smaller") are Rive's own marketing claims — flagged `verify: true` since they were not independently re-tested. Useful for Week 9's tradeoffs discussion; pair with a neutral source in class discussion if possible.
