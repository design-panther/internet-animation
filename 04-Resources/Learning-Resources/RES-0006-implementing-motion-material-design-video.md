## What it covers
Part of Google Design's official tutorial series, this short video walks through Material Design's motion principles in practice — how duration, easing, and choreography communicate hierarchy and continuity between screens, illustrated with real Android/Material UI examples.

## What you'll learn
- How Material Design's motion curves (easing) differ from linear animation
- Why consistent motion timing across an interface builds trust and clarity
- How to "read" a transition for whether it's guiding attention or just decorating
- Vocabulary (duration, easing, choreography) useful for critiquing animation later in the course
