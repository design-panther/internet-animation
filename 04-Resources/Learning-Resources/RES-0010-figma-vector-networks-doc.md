## What it covers
Figma's official explanation of "vector networks" — its underlying model for vector shape layers, which is more flexible than a traditional single-direction path because multiple segments can branch, merge, and intersect from one point. Covers using the Pen tool (P) to place points, drag Bezier handles for curves, and close or leave paths open, plus the six end-cap styling options.

## What you'll learn
- What makes Figma's vector model different from Illustrator's traditional path model
- How to draw and edit points/curves with Figma's Pen tool
- How to open vs. close a path, and how to style open-path end caps
- The foundational vocabulary needed before building animation-ready icons/shapes in Figma
