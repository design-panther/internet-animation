## What it covers
NN/g's foundational overview of usability testing: what it is, why it works, and the roles involved (facilitator, tasks, participants). It contrasts qualitative vs. quantitative testing and remote vs. in-person formats, and discusses the range of study formats from quick "discount" tests to elaborate research projects.

## What you'll learn
- The core recipe for a usability test: representative users, representative tasks, and observation
- The difference between qualitative (find problems) and quantitative (measure) testing
- Remote moderated vs. unmoderated vs. in-person tradeoffs
- How to scope a test to fit a limited budget and timeline
- Vocabulary you'll need for the rest of the unit (facilitator, task, think-aloud)

## Notes
Foundational reading for Week 11's usability testing unit. Establishes vocabulary and the general method before students adapt these techniques specifically to motion/animation (preference and comprehension tests).
