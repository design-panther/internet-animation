## What it covers
Apple's official guidance on using motion across its platforms: using motion to communicate status and feedback, keeping people oriented, avoiding gratuitous or excessive animation, and — critically — making motion optional so people who have turned on reduced-motion settings aren't blocked from understanding what happened.

## What you'll learn
- Apple's core rule that motion should never be the *only* way information is communicated
- Guidance on using animation "judiciously" to avoid disconnecting or distracting people
- How to design custom motion that keeps users oriented rather than surprising them
- Platform-level respect for user motion preferences as a baseline expectation, not an edge case
