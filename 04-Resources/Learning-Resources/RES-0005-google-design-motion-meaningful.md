## What it covers
Google Design profiles several of its own designers (working on Google Photos, YouTube Kids, Google Duo, and Android) explaining how each team used motion with intent rather than decoration — from "motion sleight-of-hand" that hides complexity in Google Photos, to exaggerated, connective timing in Duo, to a bouncy, personality-driven Android boot animation.

## What you'll learn
- Concrete, real-product examples of motion solving a UX problem (not just looking nice)
- The idea of "motion sleight-of-hand" for simplifying visually busy transitions
- Why different audiences (e.g., kids vs. professionals) call for different motion tones
- Why motion design succeeds only through collaboration between designers, animators, and engineers

## Notes
Core Week 1 reading for "motion with purpose vs. decoration" — have students name which of the four examples (Photos, YouTube Kids, Duo, Android) most resembles the app they use daily, and why.
