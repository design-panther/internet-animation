## What it covers
A practical, non-hype rundown of AI tools that are genuinely useful in a motion designer's workflow today, including Adobe Firefly (generative fill), With Poly (seamless textures), Rokoko Video (motion capture from ordinary video), Eleven Labs (text-to-speech/voiceover), and DiffusAE (in-app image generation for After Effects), while arguing that "humans are still going to make better art than computers."

## What you'll learn
- Specific, current AI tools motion designers use for generative fill, textures, mocap, and voiceover
- Where AI currently speeds up production (assets, prototyping) vs. where it doesn't replace human judgment
- A grounded, skeptical framework for evaluating whether a new AI tool is worth adopting
- How this connects to the earlier platform comparisons (AI-generated assets still need to be exported/optimized as SVG, Lottie, Rive, etc.)
