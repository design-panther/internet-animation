## What it covers
Takes the classic 12 principles of animation and maps each one directly onto interface design decisions — for example connecting "anticipation" to hover/pressed states, "follow-through" to overshoot on a dismissed panel, and "timing" to how fast a menu should open. Written for designers, not animators, with UI-specific examples throughout.

## What you'll learn
- How each of the 12 classical principles has a direct UI equivalent (button feedback, loading states, transitions)
- Why "staging" (directing attention) matters as much on a screen as on a film frame
- How exaggeration and squash-and-stretch translate to subtle UI feedback, not cartoonish motion
- Practical vocabulary for critiquing your own interface animations
