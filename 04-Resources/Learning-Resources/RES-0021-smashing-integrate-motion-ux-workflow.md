## What it covers
A practical framework, by a UX architect, for treating motion as a design deliverable that goes through the same process as any other UX artifact: Discover & Explore (tie animation ideas to real user research/needs), Design & Storyboard, Prototype, and Test with Users. Emphasizes justifying motion by the user need it serves (directing focus, feedback, clarifying hierarchy) rather than by taste alone.

## What you'll learn
- A four-stage process for planning motion that starts from user research, not from a mood board
- How to justify a proposed animation to stakeholders by tying it to a specific user need
- Why storyboarding motion concepts helps a team communicate before anyone opens a prototyping tool
- The importance of testing motion with real users rather than assuming it "feels right"

## Notes
Supports Week 4's "writing a motion brief" and User-Centered Design topics: the Discover & Explore stage maps directly onto identifying audience, purpose, and context of use before defining success criteria for a motion brief.
