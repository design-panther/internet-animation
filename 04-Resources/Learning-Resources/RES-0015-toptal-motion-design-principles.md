## What it covers
A 12-principle framework built specifically for digital motion design (as opposed to the classical animation set): easing, offset and delay, parenting, transformation, value change, masking, overlay, cloning, obscuration, parallax, dimensionality, and dolly/zoom. Each principle is illustrated with UI-relevant descriptions of what it looks like and why it's used.

## What you'll learn
- A UI-native vocabulary of motion techniques that goes beyond the classical 12 principles
- How "offset and delay" (staggering) communicates hierarchy among grouped elements
- The distinction the author draws between motion design (behavioral, purposeful) and decorative UI animation (cosmetic)
- How easing curves shape whether motion reads as natural or mechanical
