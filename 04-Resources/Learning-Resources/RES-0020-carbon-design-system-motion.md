## What it covers
IBM's Carbon Design System motion guidelines, which split motion into two named categories: "productive" motion (efficient, subtle, used for task-focused microinteractions like buttons and dropdowns) and "expressive" motion (vibrant, highly visible, used for significant moments like page transitions or alerts). Also covers duration scaling by distance/size and warns against unnatural easing (bounce, stretch, sudden stops).

## What you'll learn
- A practical two-category framework (productive vs. expressive) for deciding how much motion a given interaction deserves
- Why Carbon explicitly avoids bounce/stretch/elastic easing in a business/productivity context
- How duration should scale with the size of the on-screen change
- How an enterprise design system documents motion for a large team of distributed contributors
