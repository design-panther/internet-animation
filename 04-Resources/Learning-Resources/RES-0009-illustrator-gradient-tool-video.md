## What it covers
A practical, screen-recorded walkthrough of Illustrator's Gradient tool, covering every option in the Gradient panel: creating linear and radial gradients, adding/removing/repositioning color stops, adjusting angle and opacity of stops, and applying gradients to both fills and strokes.

## What you'll learn
- How to build and edit linear vs. radial gradients from scratch
- How to add gradients to a stroke, not just a fill
- How to control the direction and spread of a gradient with the Gradient tool directly on the artboard
- Enough gradient fluency to prep artwork with the layered fills/strokes needed for later Lottie/Rive export
