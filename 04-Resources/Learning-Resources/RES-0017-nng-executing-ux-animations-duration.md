## What it covers
A Nielsen Norman Group article giving concrete duration ranges for UI animation: roughly 100-500ms depending on complexity and distance traveled, with specific guidance for simple feedback (~100ms), modals (200-300ms), and large-scale movements (up to ~400ms). Explains why entering elements often need slightly longer durations than exiting ones, and why animations more often err on being too long than too short.

## What you'll learn
- Concrete millisecond ranges to start from when timing your own UI animations
- Why a 50ms difference in duration is perceptible to users
- The asymmetry between "entering" and "exiting" animation timing
- A rule of thumb: when in doubt, favor shorter durations
