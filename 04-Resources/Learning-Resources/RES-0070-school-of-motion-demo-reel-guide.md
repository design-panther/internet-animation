## What it covers
Practical advice for building a motion design demo reel as a focused "argument for why someone should hire you" rather than an exhaustive archive. It covers targeting a specialty, opening with the strongest work, keeping the reel to roughly 20-60 seconds, crediting collaborators honestly, and getting outside feedback before publishing.

## What you'll learn
- How to select and sequence clips so the reel opens strong
- How to keep a reel tight (20-60 seconds) instead of a highlight dump
- How to credit team/collaborative work honestly
- Why and how to get critical feedback before calling a reel "done"
