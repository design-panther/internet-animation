## What it covers
A walkthrough of Dan Saffer's original four-component model of a microinteraction: **trigger** (what starts it — user action or system event), **rules** (what happens and in what sequence), **feedback** (how the result is communicated — visual, auditory, or haptic), and **loops and modes** (how the interaction repeats or changes over time). It applies the model to a familiar example, a social-media "like" button, and adds practical design steps, common pitfalls, and evaluation methods like cognitive walkthroughs and A/B testing.

## What you'll learn
- Saffer's full four-part structural model for designing any microinteraction
- How to trace a single interaction (e.g., a "like" button) through all four stages
- Common pitfalls when designing triggers, rules, feedback, or loops
- How to evaluate a microinteraction design with a cognitive walkthrough

## Notes
