## What it covers
Figma's official guide to two core ways of organizing layers: Groups (Cmd/Ctrl+G), which auto-resize to fit their contents and can be nested infinitely, versus Frames (F or Cmd/Ctrl+Alt+G), which have explicitly set dimensions and can carry their own properties (fills, corner radius, auto layout, constraints).

## What you'll learn
- When to reach for a group vs. a frame while building artwork
- Why frames (not groups) are the right container for anything needing fixed bounds, auto layout, or constraints
- How nesting groups/frames creates the layer hierarchy that animation tools later read
- Practical shortcuts for grouping, framing, and ungrouping quickly

## Notes
Directly supports Week 2's "building animation-ready artwork (layer organization, naming, groups)" topic — emphasize in class that a clean, well-named, correctly grouped/framed layer tree in Figma or Illustrator is what makes later Lottie/Rive export actually work.
