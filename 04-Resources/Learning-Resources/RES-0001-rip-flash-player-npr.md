## What it covers
This NPR news piece marks the official end of Adobe Flash Player on January 1, 2021, explaining why Adobe pulled the plug (security flaws, the rise of HTML5, mobile devices never supporting it) and what Flash meant to a generation of web creativity — from games to animated cartoons to interactive ads.

## What you'll learn
- Why Flash dominated web animation for roughly two decades before its decline
- The practical and security reasons Adobe discontinued it
- How the "death of Flash" pushed the industry toward open web standards (HTML5, CSS, SVG, JS)
- Why this moment matters as the starting point for understanding today's vector-animation tools (Lottie, Rive)
