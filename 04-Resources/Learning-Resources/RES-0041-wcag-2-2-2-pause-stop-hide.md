## What it covers
The official W3C explanation of WCAG Success Criterion 2.2.2 (Level A): any moving, blinking, scrolling, or auto-updating content that starts automatically, lasts more than five seconds, and is presented alongside other content must have a mechanism for users to pause, stop, or hide it (unless the motion is essential to the activity). It explains the rationale — people with cognitive disabilities and attention deficits can be distracted by continuous movement — and why five seconds was chosen as the threshold.

## What you'll learn
- The exact wording and scope of WCAG 2.2.2
- Which kinds of animated/auto-updating content trigger this requirement
- Why and how to add pause/stop/hide controls to looping or autoplaying animation
- The "essential" exception and how to reason about it
