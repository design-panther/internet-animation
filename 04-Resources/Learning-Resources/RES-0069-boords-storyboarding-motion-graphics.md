## What it covers
A comprehensive, free guide to storyboarding specifically for motion graphics projects: understanding plot/story beats, basic character design, incorporating dialogue/narration, common tools, a step-by-step creation process, and common mistakes beginners make.

## What you'll learn
- Why storyboarding is the pre-production "visual blueprint" for a motion project
- How to break a script or concept into a sequence of key frames/beats
- How to note timing, transitions, and camera moves on a storyboard panel
- Common beginner mistakes to avoid (over-detailing panels, skipping timing notes, etc.)

## Notes
Core reading for Week 13's storyboarding/animatics unit for the final project. Students should leave able to draft a storyboard that a partner could animate from without further explanation.
