## What it covers
A beginner-friendly walkthrough of creating and working with shape layers in After Effects, demonstrating the shape tools, the Pen tool, and the fill/stroke/path structure of a shape layer inside the interface.

## What you'll learn
- How to draw a shape layer with the built-in shape tools and the Pen tool
- How the Contents panel exposes a shape's path, fill, and stroke as separately editable properties
- Basic navigation of the shape layer property groups in the Timeline panel
- A hands-on companion to the conceptual overview in Adobe's official docs
