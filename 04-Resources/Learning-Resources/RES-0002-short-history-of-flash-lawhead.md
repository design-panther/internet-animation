## What it covers
A first-hand, insider account by an independent artist/developer who worked through the Flash era, describing websites as "the new emerging artform," the creative agencies and hobbyists who pushed the medium forward, and her argument that Flash's decline was driven by platform politics (Apple/App Store gatekeeping) as much as by technical obsolescence.

## What you'll learn
- What made Flash-era websites feel like an art movement rather than just software
- How Flash lowered the barrier for independent creators to publish rich interactive/animated work
- A counter-narrative to the "Flash was just insecure and slow" story students usually hear
- Why preserving/understanding creative-web history matters for designers working in today's walled-garden platforms
