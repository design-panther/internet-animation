## What it covers
A visual catalog of interaction patterns organized by the exact set of states this course targets: element states (default, hover, focus, disabled), **loading** patterns (spinners, step indicators, progress notifications), **success** confirmations (checkmarks, toasts, banners), **error** messages (what went wrong, in plain language, with a fix), and **empty** states (pre-populated vs. action-oriented approaches). It also zooms out to page-level flows and system-wide interactions and reminds designers to account for edge cases (CRUD operations, zero-data states).

## What you'll learn
- A concrete example for each of the four microinteraction states: loading, success, error, empty
- How to choose the right loading UI for a given wait time (spinner vs. progress vs. skeleton)
- What makes an error message actionable rather than just alarming
- How to design an empty state that teaches or prompts action instead of just showing nothing
