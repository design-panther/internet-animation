## What it covers
Google's current (Material 3) motion guidelines: the philosophy behind Material's physics-inspired motion, its easing tokens (standard, emphasized), duration scales, and the transition patterns (container transform, shared axis, fade through, fade) used to move between UI states and screens.

## What you'll learn
- How a major production design system defines and documents its motion language
- The difference between "standard" and "emphasized" easing and when each is used
- Named transition patterns you can reuse instead of inventing bespoke animations for every screen change
- How duration scales with the size/distance of what's animating
