## What it covers
A short NN/g video explaining the 5-second test: showing a design for just five seconds to gauge a viewer's first impression, without warning them ahead of time so their reaction is authentic rather than an over-studied read of the details.

## What you'll learn
- How to structure a 5-second test (show briefly, then ask recall/impression questions)
- Why NOT warning participants about the time limit matters for honest results
- What a 5-second test is good for (impressions, brand feel) vs. NOT good for (task completion)
- How to adapt the method to gauge a viewer's first read of an animated intro or motion sequence

## Notes
Directly maps to the course's "5-second style methods adapted to motion" for Week 11 — students can test whether a 5-second freeze-frame or short animation loop communicates the intended feeling/message on first viewing.
