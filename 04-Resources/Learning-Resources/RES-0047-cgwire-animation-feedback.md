## What it covers
A practical framework for critiquing animation work, built around Non-Violent Communication (NVC): naming concrete **observations** (specific technical issues, not vague reactions), acknowledging **feelings** the work raises, articulating the underlying **needs** at stake, and ending with an actionable **request**. It cautions against the "feedback sandwich" as potentially insincere and stresses separating critique of the work from critique of the person, with concrete, specific language over general judgments.

## What you'll learn
- A four-step (observation → feeling → need → request) framework for giving critique
- How to phrase specific, actionable feedback on timing, spacing, and motion instead of vague reactions ("I don't like it")
- Why the classic "compliment sandwich" can undermine trust in a critique session
- How to keep animation critique focused on the work, not the person who made it
