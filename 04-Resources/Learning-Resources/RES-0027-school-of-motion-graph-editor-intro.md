## What it covers
A free, plain-language walkthrough of the Graph Editor basics — how animation curves control timing and speed, how to separate dimensions to edit properties individually, and how to shape curves for effects like a bounce or an impact "wave."

## What you'll learn
- Why the Graph Editor exists and what problem it solves versus eyeballing keyframes on the Timeline
- How to separate a property's dimensions (e.g., X and Y position) for finer curve control
- How to build a believable bounce or impact motion using curve manipulation
- Practical mental models for reading a curve's shape as "speed," not just "position"
