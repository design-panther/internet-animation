## What it covers
A clear walkthrough of Disney animators Frank Thomas and Ollie Johnston's original 12 principles of animation (squash and stretch, anticipation, staging, timing, follow-through, easing, arcs, exaggeration, appeal, and more), each illustrated with classic animation clips and plain-language explanations.

## What you'll learn
- The full vocabulary of the 12 classical animation principles before they get translated to interface motion
- Why timing and spacing (not just what moves, but how fast and with what rhythm) reads as "natural" to a viewer
- How anticipation and follow-through create readable, believable motion
- A shared reference point for terms the rest of the unit reuses (easing, arcs, exaggeration)

## Notes
Supports Week 3's opening topic on translating the 12 principles to UI motion. Watch this before RES-0014, which applies the same principles specifically to interface design.
