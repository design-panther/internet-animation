# Project 1 — Animated Vector Asset Kit · 50 pts · due Week 4

Design a small set of related vector assets, animate them with **intentional
easing**, and deliver them with a **one-page motion brief**. Built across the
whole of Unit 1: artwork in Week 2, animation in Week 3, brief in Week 4.

## Deliverables

1. **The asset set** — 3–5 related vector assets (an icon set, a mascot's poses, UI elements for one imagined product). Clean paths, organized and named layers, animation-ready.
2. **The animations** — each asset animated. Every ease must have a reason; be ready to say what `ease-out` on that element is *doing for the user*.
3. **The motion brief** — one page, using [[Motion-Brief-Template]]: audience, purpose, success criteria.

Deliver source files (AI/Figma + your animation tool's file) plus rendered
previews (GIF/MP4/Lottie — any format that plays).

## Constraints

- Vector only. If it's a raster effect, it doesn't belong in this project.
- The set must be *related* — one visual system, not three orphans.

## Grading (50 pts)

- 15 pts — craft of the vector artwork (paths, structure, layer hygiene)
- 15 pts — animation quality (timing, easing, principle-informed choices)
- 10 pts — the motion brief (specific audience, purpose, testable success criteria)
- 10 pts — participation: use of work sessions and the Week-4 brief peer-swap
