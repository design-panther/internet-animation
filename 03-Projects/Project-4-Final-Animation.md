# Project 4 — Final Animation + UCD Process Story · 50 pts · due Week 16

The capstone: a self-proposed animation project taken through the **full
User-Centered Design cycle** — proposal, persona, motion brief, storyboard,
production, peer usability test, revision, optimization, and presentation.

## Milestones

| Week | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 12 | **Proposal due** — persona, purpose, platform, scope (one page) |
| 13 | Approved proposal → motion brief + storyboard/animatic; instructor check-in |
| 14 | Production; **mid-production peer usability test** (Week-11 method) |
| 15 | Revision from findings; optimization pass (file size, reduced-motion fallback); portfolio packaging |
| 16 | **FINAL PRESENTATIONS** |

## Deliverables

1. **The animation(s)** — on the platform your brief justified (Lottie, Rive, Fancy Animation Studio, or web-native SVG/CSS). Scope agreed at proposal.
2. **The process story** — brief → test → revision, presented in ~5 minutes. The story is graded, not just the file.
3. **The package** — a LottieFiles profile, demo page, or equivalent a stranger could visit.
4. **The optimization evidence** — file-size numbers and the reduced-motion fallback.

## Grading (50 pts)

- 15 pts — craft and ambition of the finished piece
- 10 pts — the UCD process actually happened (brief, test, traceable revision)
- 10 pts — technical delivery and optimization (incl. reduced-motion fallback)
- 5 pts — the package (presentable to a stranger)
- 10 pts — presentation and final-critique participation
