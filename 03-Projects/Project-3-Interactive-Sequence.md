# Project 3 — Interactive or Extended Sequence · 50 pts · due Week 11

Build **either** an interactive animation in **Rive** (state machine with at
least two inputs) **or** an extended staged sequence in **Fancy Animation
Studio** (10+ seconds, multiple motion-path types) — then **test it with 3
users** and make one documented revision.

## Deliverables

1. **The piece** — a working Rive file/embed (state machine, inputs, listeners) *or* a Fancy Animation Studio composition (staged sequence using at least three motion-path types).
2. **The test plan and findings** — using [[Usability-Test-Kit]]: what you asked, who your 3 users were (classmates fine), what you observed, what you concluded (without over-claiming from n=3).
3. **The revision** — before/after of one concrete change driven by the findings, and why.
4. **Platform justification** — three sentences: why this platform for this piece ([[Platform-Comparison]]).

## Grading (50 pts)

- 15 pts — craft and ambition of the piece
- 10 pts — interactivity/staging actually works (states respond; sequence reads)
- 10 pts — the user test: sound method, honest findings
- 10 pts — the revision is real and traceable to a finding
- 5 pts — platform justification
