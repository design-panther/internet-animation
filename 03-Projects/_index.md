# Projects & Exercises

Four 50-point projects and two 10-point exercises — 220 points total (see
[[Grading]]). Every project is graded on four things:

1. **Meeting the assignment objectives** — did you build what the brief asks for?
2. **Participation** — use of class time and critique.
3. **Craft** — innovative use and craft of the digital media.
4. **UCD rationale** — can you say who the motion is for, what it's supposed to do, and how you know?
