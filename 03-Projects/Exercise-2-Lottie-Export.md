# Exercise 2 — Working Lottie Export · 10 pts · due Week 6

Export a working Lottie animation from After Effects with Bodymovin and **test
it on two targets**: a web page and a phone preview.

## Deliverable

1. The **.json / .lottie file**, exported from your own AE composition (the Week-5 shape-layer piece is fine).
2. **Proof of two targets** — a screenshot/recording of it playing (a) embedded in a web page (the dotLottie player or LottieFiles preview page counts) and (b) on a phone (LottieFiles mobile preview).
3. **Three sentences** on what survived export and what didn't — name at least one AE feature you learned is unsupported.

## Grading (10 pts)

- 5 pts — the export works (plays correctly, vector, reasonable size)
- 3 pts — both targets shown
- 2 pts — the supported/unsupported observation is real and specific
